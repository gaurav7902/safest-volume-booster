**Volume Booster** gives you a simple popup for increasing audio and video volume above the normal browser limit.

Use the slider to raise volume from **100%** up to **600%**, choose a quick preset, disable boosting with one toggle, or reset everything back to the default level.

Features:

- Boost audio/video volume from 100% to 600%
- Quick preset buttons for 100%, 200%, 400%, and 600%
- Popup toggle to enable or disable boosting
- Reset button for returning to normal volume
- Works through a content script on pages with standard audio or video elements

**Privacy:** This extension stores only your local volume preference and enabled/disabled state in browser extension storage. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share user data. It does not use analytics, tracking scripts, external API calls, or remote code.
